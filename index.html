About Vladyslav

Vladyslav Orlov (born 17 May 1995) is a Ukrainian professional tennis player representing his country on the international stage, including the Davis Cup.

With a career-high ATP singles ranking of World No. 350 and multiple ITF titles to his name, Vladyslav is known for his resilience and tactical gameplay on both hard and clay courts.

  • Residence: Kharkiv, Ukraine
